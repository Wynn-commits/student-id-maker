Dormitory Rules

In order to maintain the cleanliness and orderliness of Elpueblo Dormitory, Dormers are expected to follow the Dorm Rules as listed below:


  1. Within The Premises
  • Strictly No Smoking
  • No drinking of alcoholic beverages
  • Ensure door is always secured/locked
  1. Room
  • No Visitors/outsider (Not roommates) allowed inside the room
  • Please do not bring food and drinks inside room to avoid infestation of cockroaches and rats
  • Practice CLAYGO (Clean As You Go)
  • Switch off lights and Wall/Ceiling fan when not in use
  • Practice Common Courtesy and Respect of individual space
  • Plug off elctrical devices and safely keep electrical cords away from walkways when not in use
  • To avoid theft or loss of personal belongings, please make sure that they are placed inside your room and property locked
  • Dormitory Management reserves the right to inspect room
  1. Toilet and Bathroom
  • Please clean up after use. Remove fallen hair from blocking the drainage, throw away trash in the waste bin of the bathroom area
  • Not allowed to wash your clothes inside to a shower room
  • Switch off water and light when not in use
  1. Raising issue and concerns
  • Please do not hesitate to raise any Issues/Concerns/Worries regarding the dormitory or fellow roommates to the management
